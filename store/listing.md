## Full description (max 4000)
Saral File Manager is a simple, private file manager from Saral Techies. Browse your phone's storage, SD card and USB drives, copy and move files, make and open zip files, find anything with search, and clean up what you don't need. There are no ads, you don't need an account, and the app doesn't even have internet access, so your files never leave your phone. It's free and open source.

SEE EVERYTHING AT A GLANCE
• Internal storage, SD card and USB drives, with how much space is used
• Categories for images, videos, audio, documents, archives and APKs
• Quick access to Downloads, Camera and Documents
• Bookmark the folders you use most
• Recent files from the last 30 days

BROWSE YOUR WAY
• List or grid view, with photo and video previews
• Sort by name, date, size or type, in either order
• Names sort naturally: "Photo 2" comes before "Photo 10"
• Show or hide hidden files
• A path bar to jump back to any folder

MANAGE FILES
• Copy, cut and paste between folders and storages
• Rename, delete, create folders and files
• Select many items with a long press, or select all
• See details: location, date, type, and the size of whole folders
• Open files in your favourite apps, or share them to WhatsApp, email and more
• Copying and moving run with a progress bar, and you can cancel any time

ZIP AND UNZIP
• Compress files and folders into a zip
• Tap a zip to extract it into a new folder

SEARCH
• Find files and folders by name in any folder and everything inside it, as you type

PRIVATE AND OPEN
• No ads and no sign-up
• No internet permission: the app can't send your files anywhere
• Free and open source under the GPL-3.0 licence: github.com/saraltechies/saralfilemanager
• Light and dark themes

Made in India by Saral Techies (saraltechies.com).
