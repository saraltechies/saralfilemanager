SaralFileManager Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

SaralFileManager ("the app") is a file manager for Android, published by Saral Techies, Amroha, Uttar Pradesh, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and how. In short: the app does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal data. It has no internet access.

1. Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting SDKs. It does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send data to Saral Techies or any third party.

2. Data processed on your device

DataWhyWhere it stays
Your files and folders (names, sizes, dates and contents)To show, copy, move, rename, delete, zip, extract and search them when you askOn your device. The app works on the files where they are and never uploads them. Photo and video previews are made on the phone and kept only in memory while the app is open.
Settings (sort order, list or grid view, hidden files) and bookmarked foldersTo remember your preferencesStored only in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

3. Permissions

PermissionWhy
All files access (Android 11 and newer)A file manager has to list and change every file and folder in your phone's shared storage, SD card and USB drives. You turn it on yourself in Android's settings and can turn it off there at any time.
Storage read and write (Android 8 to 10)The same, on older Android versions.

The app uses this access only to do what you ask on screen. It does not scan, read or send your files in the background.

4. Opening and sharing files

When you choose "Open with" or "Share", the file goes only to the app you pick, which handles it under its own privacy policy. The Rate, More apps, Source code and Privacy policy items open Google Play or your own web browser.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not share, sell or rent any data, because we do not collect any.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

7. Security and source code

Settings stay in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. The app is open source, so anyone can check what it does: github.com/saraltechies/saralfilemanager.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. If the app ever starts collecting data, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety section before that version is released.
